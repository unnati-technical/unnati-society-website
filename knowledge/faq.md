# Frequently Asked Questions — Unnati Society

## Volunteering FAQs

**Q: Is volunteering with Unnati paid?**
A: No, volunteering with Unnati Society is entirely unpaid. However, it is an incredibly rewarding experience — you gain real teaching experience, build skills, grow your network, and receive a Certificate of Appreciation from IIIT Bhagalpur.

**Q: Do I get a certificate for volunteering?**
A: Yes! Active volunteers who complete their commitment receive an official Certificate of Appreciation issued by IIIT Bhagalpur. This certificate is recognized and valued in academic and professional settings.

**Q: What is the minimum age to volunteer?**
A: We primarily welcome college students aged 18 and above as teaching volunteers. High school students aged 16+ can participate in event assistance and support roles.

**Q: How much time do I need to commit?**
A: It varies by role. Most teaching volunteer roles require 2-5 hours per week. Event coordinators contribute on an event basis. We try to accommodate flexible schedules for students and working professionals.

**Q: Can I volunteer online?**
A: Yes! Some roles like content creation, social media management, and online tutoring can be done remotely. Other roles like classroom teaching require physical presence at partner schools.

**Q: What skills do I need to volunteer?**
A: It depends on the role. Teaching roles need patience and communication skills. Technical roles (DigiXplore) need basic computer knowledge. Media roles need photography/design skills. We welcome volunteers from all backgrounds!

**Q: How do I apply to volunteer?**
A: You can apply through the Volunteer section on our website, or start the application process right here through the chatbot. We'll match you with the most suitable role based on your skills and availability.

## Donation FAQs

**Q: How can I donate to Unnati Society?**
A: You can make a donation directly through our website's Donate section. We accept online payments through our secure payment gateway.

**Q: Is my donation tax-deductible?**
A: Please contact us directly for information about tax exemption status and 80G certificates.

**Q: What does my donation go towards?**
A: Donations fund teaching materials, computer equipment for DigiXplore, event costs, transportation for volunteers, and operational costs of running our programs.

**Q: Is there a minimum donation amount?**
A: No minimum! Every contribution, big or small, makes a difference.

## School Partnership FAQs

**Q: How can our school partner with Unnati?**
A: Schools can apply through the School Partnership or Outreach Request forms on our website. A coordinator will review your request and reach out within a few working days.

**Q: Is there a cost for schools to participate?**
A: No, Unnati's programs are provided completely free of charge to partner schools. Our goal is to remove all barriers to quality education.

**Q: Which districts does Unnati operate in?**
A: We primarily operate in Bhagalpur district and surrounding areas in Bihar. We are actively expanding to new districts.

**Q: What programs can our school access?**
A: Depending on your school's needs and grade levels, you can access DigiXplore (digital literacy), Akshar (primary education), and Netritva (leadership for Classes 9-12).

## General FAQs

**Q: Is Unnati a government organization?**
A: No, Unnati Society is a student-led NGO operating as an institute outreach activity of IIIT Bhagalpur. It is not directly affiliated with the state or central government.

**Q: How can I contact Unnati?**
A: You can contact us through the Contact Us form on our website. You can also reach out via our social media channels.

**Q: Can I visit the IIIT Bhagalpur campus?**
A: Yes! We organize campus visits for school groups. You can apply through the Campus Visit form on our website.

**Q: Does Unnati organize events?**
A: Yes! We regularly organize workshops, seminars, competitions, and outreach events. Check the Events section of our website or ask me for upcoming events.
