# Volunteering with Unnati Society — Complete Guide

## Why Volunteer with Unnati?

Volunteering with Unnati Society gives you a chance to:
- **Make real impact** — directly teach and mentor underprivileged children
- **Build skills** — communication, leadership, planning, and technical skills
- **Get certified** — receive an official Certificate of Appreciation from IIIT Bhagalpur
- **Network** — connect with passionate students and social sector professionals
- **Add to your resume/CV** — NGO experience is highly valued in placements and higher education

---

## Volunteer Roles Available

### Teaching Volunteers (DigiXplore)
- Teach computer basics, internet skills, and digital literacy to school children
- Skill needed: Basic computer knowledge, patience, communication
- Time: 3-4 hours per week, offline at partner schools

### Teaching Volunteers (Akshar)
- Teach reading, writing, and basic maths to primary school children
- Skill needed: Hindi/English literacy, creativity, patience
- Time: 3-4 hours per week, offline

### Mentorship Leads (Netritva)
- Conduct workshops on career, leadership, and financial literacy for Class 9-12 students
- Skill needed: Communication, life experience, ability to inspire
- Time: 2-3 hours per week or event-based

### Event Coordinators
- Organize and manage Unnati events — logistics, scheduling, venue, communication
- Skill needed: Organization, communication, multitasking
- Time: Event-based (flexible)

### Content Creators (Online Role)
- Create educational content, infographics, videos, social media posts
- Skill needed: Design tools (Canva, Photoshop), video editing, writing
- Time: 2-3 hours per week, fully online

### Technical Volunteers (Online/Offline)
- Maintain computers, set up labs, support DigiXplore sessions
- Skill needed: Computer hardware/software basics
- Time: As needed, flexible

## How to Apply

**Step 1**: Visit our website and navigate to the Volunteer section, OR ask the chatbot to start your application.

**Step 2**: Fill out the volunteer application form with:
- Your personal details and contact information
- Your educational background and current institution
- Your skills and interests
- Your availability (days, hours per week)
- The program/role you are interested in
- Why you want to volunteer with Unnati

**Step 3**: Our team will review your application and contact you within 3-5 business days.

**Step 4**: Attend an orientation session to learn about Unnati's mission, programs, and guidelines.

**Step 5**: Begin your volunteer journey!

## Volunteer Code of Conduct

All Unnati volunteers are expected to:
1. Be punctual and reliable — students depend on your presence
2. Be respectful and inclusive — treat all children with dignity
3. Maintain confidentiality of student information
4. Follow Unnati's program guidelines and curriculum
5. Represent Unnati positively in the community
6. Report any concerns to the program lead immediately

---

## Certificate of Appreciation

Active volunteers who fulfill their commitment period receive an official Certificate of Appreciation:
- Issued by: IIIT Bhagalpur
- Signed by: Program leads and faculty advisors
- Content: Your name, role, duration, and contribution
- Format: Digital (PDF) and optionally physical
- Verification: Each certificate has a unique reference number for verification
