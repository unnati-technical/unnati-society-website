# Unnati Society Programs

Unnati Society runs three core educational programs, each targeting a different need and age group.

---

## Program 1: DigiXplore (Digital Literacy Program)

**Full Name**: DigiXplore — Digital Exploration

**What It Is**: DigiXplore is Unnati's flagship digital literacy program aimed at teaching school students the fundamentals of computers and digital technology. It bridges the digital divide for children who have little or no access to technology at home.

**What Students Learn**:
- Basic computer operation (on/off, keyboard, mouse, files)
- Internet browsing and online safety
- Microsoft Office basics (Word, Excel, PowerPoint)
- Email and digital communication
- Introduction to coding concepts
- Online government services awareness

**Target Audience**: Students from Classes 5 to 10 in partner schools, primarily in rural and semi-urban Bihar.

**Mode**: Offline, conducted in school computer labs or with Unnati-provided equipment.

**Volunteer Roles in DigiXplore**:
- Computer Lab Instructor
- Technical Support Volunteer
- Curriculum Assistant

---

## Program 2: Akshar (Basic Literacy & Primary Education)

**Full Name**: Akshar — The Letter (symbolizing the power of literacy)

**What It Is**: Akshar is Unnati's primary education and basic literacy initiative. It focuses on foundational literacy and numeracy skills for underprivileged children who are either struggling in school or have dropped out.

**What Students Learn**:
- Reading and writing in Hindi and basic English
- Basic arithmetic and mathematics
- Story-based learning and creative activities
- Life skills and personal hygiene education

**Target Audience**: Children from Classes 1 to 5, especially from economically weaker sections.

**Mode**: Offline, in school classrooms or community centers.

**Volunteer Roles in Akshar**:
- Primary School Teacher
- Story and Activity Coordinator
- Learning Material Creator

---

## Program 3: Netritva (Leadership & Career Development)

**Full Name**: Netritva — Leadership (from Sanskrit/Hindi)

**What It Is**: Netritva is Unnati's leadership, career guidance, and financial literacy program for older students. It prepares high schoolers for their future by building soft skills, awareness of career paths, and financial understanding.

**What Students Learn**:
- Public speaking and communication skills
- Career guidance sessions with IIIT students and alumni
- Financial literacy: savings, banking, digital payments, avoiding scams
- Entrance exam awareness (JEE, NEET, government exams)
- Goal setting and motivation workshops

**Target Audience**: Students from Classes 9 to 12.

**Mode**: Offline workshops, seminars, and interactive sessions.

**Volunteer Roles in Netritva**:
- Workshop Facilitator
- Career Counseling Volunteer
- Motivational Speaker / Mentor
