# About Unnati Society

## What is Unnati Society?

Unnati Society is a student-led Non-Governmental Organization (NGO) operating as an Institute Outreach Activity of the Indian Institute of Information Technology (IIIT) Bhagalpur. It was founded by passionate students who wanted to bridge the educational gap in rural and semi-urban areas of Bihar and beyond.

The name "Unnati" means "progress" or "upliftment" in Hindi — perfectly capturing our mission to uplift communities through education and opportunity.

## Our Mission

To empower underprivileged children and communities through quality education, digital literacy, leadership development, and holistic community engagement.

## Our Vision

To build a future where every child — regardless of their socioeconomic background — has access to basic literacy, digital skills, and the tools to thrive in the modern world.

## Our History and Founding

Unnati Society was founded by a group of dedicated students from IIIT Bhagalpur who recognized the need for quality education outreach in rural Bihar. Since its founding, Unnati has grown from a small initiative into a well-structured organization with multiple programs, hundreds of volunteers, and partnerships with numerous schools.

## What Makes Unnati Unique?

- Student-driven: Founded, managed, and run entirely by college students
- Multi-program approach: Covers digital literacy, basic education, and leadership development
- Community embedded: Works directly in schools and local communities
- Certified impact: Active volunteers receive official certificates from IIIT Bhagalpur
