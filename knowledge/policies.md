# Unnati Society Policies

## Privacy Policy

Unnati Society is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data.

### Information We Collect
- **Volunteer Applications**: Name, email, phone, educational background, skills, and availability
- **Contact Forms**: Name, email, and your message
- **Donations**: Donor name, email, contact, PAN (for tax purposes), and payment details
- **Event Registrations**: Name, contact details, and institution
- **School Partnership Requests**: Institutional details, coordinator information

### How We Use Your Information
- To process and manage volunteer applications
- To respond to contact and support requests
- To process donations and issue receipts
- To send relevant updates about Unnati programs and events (with your consent)
- To issue certificates of appreciation to volunteers

### Data Security
We use industry-standard security practices including SSL encryption. Payment processing is handled by certified payment gateways and we do not store sensitive payment card data.

### Your Rights
You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us through the website.

### Third-Party Services
We use third-party services including Cloudinary (media storage), Firebase (authentication), and payment processors. These services have their own privacy policies.

## Donation Policy

### Accepted Payment Methods
- UPI
- Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay)
- Net Banking
- Digital Wallets

### Refund Policy
Donations are generally non-refundable. In case of duplicate payments or technical errors, please contact us within 7 days and we will process a refund.

### Tax Exemption
Please contact us directly for information about tax exemption certificates under Section 80G or other applicable provisions.

### Use of Funds
All donations are used exclusively for:
- Educational materials and equipment for students
- Event organization and program delivery costs
- Volunteer support and training
- Operational costs of running Unnati's programs

## School Partnership Policy

### Eligibility
Any registered school in India can apply for a partnership with Unnati. We prioritize schools in rural and semi-urban areas with limited access to educational resources.

### What We Provide
- Free educational programs tailored to your school's needs
- Trained and supervised volunteers
- Learning materials (where available)
- Program evaluation and feedback

### What Schools Must Provide
- Safe classroom/lab space for program delivery
- Basic infrastructure (chairs, tables; computers for DigiXplore if available)
- A point of contact / school coordinator
- Consent and cooperation for program activities

### Duration
Partnerships are typically semester-long, with the option to renew and expand.

## Code of Ethics

Unnati Society operates on the following core ethical principles:

1. **Child Safety First**: All volunteers undergo orientation focused on child safeguarding. Physical punishment or verbal abuse of any kind is strictly prohibited and grounds for immediate removal.

2. **Inclusivity**: We do not discriminate based on caste, religion, gender, or economic background. All children are treated with equal respect and dignity.

3. **Transparency**: We maintain transparent operations. Donation usage and organizational activities are shared with our community.

4. **Academic Integrity**: Our educational content is developed and reviewed for accuracy. We never promote misinformation.

5. **Respect for Communities**: We work with and for communities — not on them. We respect local customs, languages, and perspectives.
